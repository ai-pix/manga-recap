# YouTube Recap Thumbnail Design Brief & Prompt Engineering Guide

> **Purpose:** This document contains the complete context, visual requirements, creative decisions, and failure analysis for generating an ultra-high-CTR YouTube recap thumbnail for **Tomb Raider King (Chapters 01–30 Full Movie)**. Share this with any AI prompt engineer or designer to generate the final image prompts.

---

## 1. Project & Story Context

- **Series:** *Tomb Raider King* (도굴왕) — Korean action/fantasy manhwa.
- **Content:** 30-chapter marathon recap video (3 hours 35 minutes runtime).
- **Protagonist:** **Seo Joo-heon**
  - **Appearance:** Handsome young Korean man, messy layered jet-black hair, glowing sharp amber/crimson eyes, dangerous arrogant half-smirk.
  - **Outfit:** Modern black tactical trench coat or streetwear jacket with sleeves rolled up to the forearms, dark compression shirt, fingerless gloves.
  - **Relics/Powers:** Wields divine ancient golden relics (winged scarab daggers, scepters, coins) crackling with cyan electric lightning runes, accompanied by a sentient glowing golden magical rope.
- **Setting:** Dark ancient subterranean tomb / pyramid ruins, cracked stone pillars, floating luminous cyan hieroglyphic seals, and heaps of glittering gold treasure chests in the gloom.

---

## 2. Core Thumbnail Philosophy (The Golden Rule)

> ⚠️ **THE CRITICAL MISTAKE TO AVOID:**  
> **Do NOT generate an image that looks like a marketing poster, a video game cover, or an AI wallpaper.**  
> Viewers have banner blindness to posed posters. They instantly skip them.

### What Actually Works:
- **"The Paused Anime Screencap":** The thumbnail MUST look like someone was watching a real, high-budget 24fps anime TV episode, paused a single chaotic/intense frame mid-action, and slapped 1–2 words and a yellow arrow on top.
- **Unstaged, Mid-Action Dynamics:** Characters are **NOT** posing for the camera. They are caught in the middle of a dynamic motion: wind whipping their hair, shockwaves blowing their clothes, a sudden grab, or a mid-air impact with natural motion blur on the edges.
- **Aspect Ratio:** Strictly **16:9 widescreen** (1280x720 or 1920x1080).

---

## 3. Character Dynamic & "Spicy" Requirement

- **Include an Attractive Anime Heroine:** YouTube recap thumbnails with an appealing female character have vastly higher CTR.
- **Appearance:** 
  - Gorgeous anime girl (silver/white hair with violet eyes, or honey-blonde with emerald eyes).
  - Fantasy adventurer outfit, priestess robe, or torn elegant dress with exposed shoulders/collarbone.
- **Expression & Tension:**
  - Highly expressive, flustered, disheveled, and heavily blushing.
  - Caught off guard, wide eyes in shock, relief, or awe.
  - Physical proximity: Held tightly against the protagonist's chest or shielded behind his arm.

---

## 4. Winning Composition: "The Effortless One-Handed Block"

The user selected **Composition 4** as the best narrative concept:
- **The Scene:** A colossal demonic boss or villain strikes downward with an immense, building-sized executioner blade.
- **The MC's Action:** The protagonist casually catches/parries the gigantic blade with a **single tiny golden relic dagger in one hand** (or his bare relic fingers).
- **The Demeanor:** Protagonist isn't even looking at the boss—he looks calm, bored, with a cold smirk. He takes **zero damage**.
- **The Heroine:** With his other arm, he is wrapping around and shielding the terrified, blushing anime heroine against his side from the shockwave.
- **Visual FX:** Blinding cyan spark explosion, golden shockwave rings at the dagger tip, atmospheric smoke and dust particles.

---

## 5. Text & Typography Rules (Strict)

1. **Keep it Ultra-Short:** Strictly **1 to 3 words maximum**. Never write full sentences or long titles.
2. **Color:** Must be **pure, electric/highlighter yellow** (`#FFE600`). In the manhwa recap niche, yellow text has the highest readability and click conversion against dark anime backgrounds.
3. **Styling:** Chunky, bold sans-serif font (Impact / Obelix style) with a **heavy black stroke/outline** and deep drop shadow for extreme contrast.
4. **Placement:** **Top-Right or Upper Center**. **NEVER bottom-right** (YouTube's timestamp overlay covers the bottom-right).
5. **High-Converting Text Ideas:**
   - `"WITH ONE HAND?!"` *(Best for disbelief/hype)*
   - `"ZERO DAMAGE."` *(Best for RPG/system min-maxing hook)*
   - `"WRONG GUY."` *(Best for revenge/underdog turning the tables)*
   - `"IMPOSSIBLE."` *(Classic shock)*
   - `"TOO LATE."` *(Urgency)*

---

## 6. The Yellow Arrow (Must-Have CTR Multiplier)

1. **Styling:** A **thick, chunky neon-yellow hand-drawn creator sticker arrow** with a **heavy black border/outline** (classic YouTube clickbait arrow style). Not a faint or thin line.
2. **Trajectory & Target:**
   - Starts near the text in the upper half of the screen.
   - Curves dynamically downward.
   - **Points directly at the focal point of absurdity:** The microscopic dagger tip casually halting the colossal 10-meter blade without a scratch.

---

## 7. Art Style: Ufotable Digital Compositing

- **Reference Studios:** **Ufotable** (*Fate/stay night: Heaven's Feel*, *Demon Slayer*).
- **Visual Signatures:**
  - Multi-layered digital photography compositing.
  - Razor-sharp, clean line art with deep contrast.
  - Intense cyan, electric blue, and radiant gold particle bloom.
  - Volumetric lighting and subtle anamorphic lens flares.
  - Cinematic 24fps film grain and natural depth-of-field blur on flying debris.

---

## 8. Summary Checklist for the Next AI Prompt Writer

When crafting prompts for Midjourney / Nano Banana Pro / DALL-E / Flux, ensure:
- [ ] Explicitly specifies: `Authentic 16:9 anime TV show screencap, paused episode freeze-frame, Ufotable animation style`.
- [ ] Action is unstaged: Protagonist parrying a massive attack with one tiny dagger while holding a blushing girl.
- [ ] Text is explicitly defined: `Top-right bold chunky electric-yellow text reading "WITH ONE HAND?!" with thick black outline`.
- [ ] Arrow is explicitly defined: `Thick chunky neon-yellow hand-drawn marker arrow with heavy black stroke, pointing directly at the tiny dagger tip stopping the colossal blade`.
- [ ] No marketing/poster buzzwords that trigger artificial 3D game covers.
